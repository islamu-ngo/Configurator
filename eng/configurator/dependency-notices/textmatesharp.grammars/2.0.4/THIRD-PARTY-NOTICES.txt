Its MIT NuGet metadata does not replace the original component terms below.

licenses from its recorded source revisions. Repeated ancestor components are
listed once per repository/revision. Repository names and source commits remain
visible. These notices do not relicense any component as Configurator material.

its exact recorded revision and original James Summerton MIT notice were recovered
from the preserved peterblazejewicz/language-cshtml repository at the same commit.

MIT notice was verified at db22e2f015a2cac3315141f40f536f3d8534c5eb (2025-07-10),
before the TextMateSharp package source snapshot. No exact asset-revision match is
claimed for that entry.

Dracula and Atom/One Dark/One Light family terms. The six contributed theme
conversions do not document exact original source revisions. Palette comparison

conservatively, without claiming byte-identical source or a unique conversion
lineage. Visual Studio Dark/Light have no identified separate license and remain

source or executable component is copied into Configurator's source tree.
