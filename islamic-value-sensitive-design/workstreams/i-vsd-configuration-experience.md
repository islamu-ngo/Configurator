## Executive Summary

Historical r1 reasoning/receipts remain input; fresh r2 review confirms the
corrected scope and mappings. r2 changes only Browser Compact: a continuous page, heading jumps,
direct-edit question rows, action panels and complete accessible visual cues.
Guided keeps its selected-section/wizard model, normal field/status treatment
and Review/Files stages. The initial r2 draft exceeded that boundary; it is
corrected before any product implementation.

Operators should be able to configure an application without first learning the editor's internal machinery. Reducing chrome and organizing the journey can benefit novice and experienced users, but convenience must not hide secret custody, incomplete drafts, lost-work risks or the limits of local validation.

Proceed with the task-first plan while retaining one typed answer authority, explicit consent, deliberate output and profile-mode restrictions. These mechanisms connect stewardship of private data to operator agency: a collapsed section cannot silently omit an answer, a quicker entry cannot consent on the user's behalf, and a clear success message cannot claim that the target application will accept prepared files.

The available evidence supports design reasoning and source traceability only. It does not establish actual keyboard/speech access, Arabic quality, current performance, reliable recovery or stakeholder outcomes. ## Findings

### IVSD-F001 - Simpler entry must preserve private custody

Operators may interpret a smoother secret/import/profile entry as permission to supply private material immediately. The provider controls consent placement, file acquisition, worker authority and export. Amanah (responsible stewardship) and people's privacy rights require custody to remain deliberate and revocable even when repeated warnings are shortened.

IVSD-M001 keeps one understandable consent destination, leaves public fields editable when secret consent is declined, and requires synchronous retirement on revocation plus generation checks after asynchronous work. Retain profile recipient/mode boundaries and deliberate export; browser clearing cannot retract downloaded/clipboard copies or promise physical string erasure. Browser/Presentation owners validate adversarial acquisition and late-result routes. Source E05-E07 supplies mechanisms, not runtime proof.

For r2, Compact panel closure retires its private capabilities while heading
jumps preserve its current form. Guided's existing Files exit rules remain.
Actual mode replacement must reject old callbacks without granting consent or
reviving preview/output. TASK-4.1/4.4 add paired-mode authority checks.

### IVSD-F002 - Progress and output can overstate readiness

Required-answer counts, complete preparation and download requests describe different facts. An operator may act on an overstated success state and deploy an incomplete configuration. Sidq (truthfulness), non-harm and promise-keeping make the provider accountable for those labels.

IVSD-M002 derives counts from selected-scope facts, separates complete/draft/blocked/invalidated output, and describes acknowledged browser requests accurately. Target validation and authorization remain with the application. Review/Files owners validate transitions and recovery; E02/E06/E16 establish the current presentation problem, not a target failure.

Compact's concise symbols/counts and optional action panels must preserve these
facts in full discoverable detail, not flatten draft/missing/invalid into a
generic success color. Guided retains its stage/text treatment.

### IVSD-F003 - Usability must include Arabic and assistive access

People using keyboard navigation, small screens, Arabic, mixed-direction technical text or assistive technology face unequal burdens if only a desktop screenshot is improved. Adl (fair access) and Ihsan (excellence) place this responsibility on the provider's choices of semantics, focus, contrast, targets and supported-host claims.

IVSD-M003 makes inclusive interaction an acceptance condition in every owning slice and requires current real-surface/AT/language evidence. Native parity uses each platform's interaction model; it does not pretend Terminal shaping or unsupported native save protection has been qualified. E03/E11/E12/E15 and W3C R02 support the design concern; participants and actual AT results remain missing.

The new visual language is Compact-only. Named icons, distinct shapes,
keyboard/touch detail, forced-color behavior and actual comprehension checks
must preserve every useful fact. Errors/essential instructions remain readable;
color or hover alone cannot carry meaning. Paired checks verify Guided's
ordinary presentation is not changed by shared CSS/parameters/policy.

### IVSD-F004 - Reduced complexity must preserve answer agency

Hiding optional detail, selecting deployment context or switching presentation can change what users see. It must not silently change what they supplied. Rights of people, avoiding deception and stewardship require typed absence/default/false/null distinctions, deliberate suggestions, cancel-safe transitions and stale-control rejection.

IVSD-M004 retains one shared mutation gate, existing pending-text resolution and row identities. Required/correction work stays reachable, and view-only controls do not materialize values or grant consent. Presentation/adapter owners establish failing new state-transition invariants before corresponding changes. E04/E05/E08 establish existing seams, not a newly proven fix.

Compact's Continuous policy must be selected atomically with layout and restore
Guided's existing Authored-or-Direct policy/experience without recreating the
form. Compact groups/filter/panels do not retire current fields; Guided section
unmounting remains a valid requirement. Root and retired-control invariants are
kept, not deleted to obtain continuity. TASK-4.1 through TASK-4.4 own this change.

### IVSD-F005 - Reuse and recovery need honest limits

Returning operators can lose work through ambiguous import/adoption, misunderstood transient sessions, unverifiable backups or unavailable recipients. The provider controls task labels, explanations, confirmation and recovery verification, but cannot reconstruct lost cryptographic keys or retract external copies. Amanah, non-harm and promise-keeping require an honest boundary.

IVSD-M005 separates comparison, adoption, opening, inspection, replacement, snapshot creation and exact saved-copy verification. Explain session lifetime before reliance on persistence. Keep worker/native mode restrictions and cancellation without inventing recovery guarantees. Browser/native profile owners validate actual focus/scroll and lifecycle outcomes; E06/E11/E12 identify mechanisms while recovery usability remains unmeasured.

### IVSD-F006 - Useful explanations and source identity are provider duties

An admitted definition can still contain confusing descriptions or misleading prerequisites. A publisher label can also be mistaken for authentication. Sidq, excellence and respect for authorship require clear maintained content, accurate source claims and independent design.

IVSD-M006 uses source-owned content revisions, bounded advisory lint, native generated identities and immutable store snapshots. Direct content work covers the maintained Event example and independent store family; reusable authoring guidance/lint supports other authors. Mechanical checks cannot certify prose or translations. TASK-1.2 and TASK-8.2 retain a source/provenance and independent-composition review: public standards inform requirements, while third-party examples, assets and distinctive UI arrangements do not enter implementation. Core/content maintainers own E09/E10 and R01-R05; target-authority or licensing decisions outside existing permission require their actual owner.

### IVSD-F007 - Responsiveness and recovery must work under constraints

Slow first use, locked cancellation, misleading busy states or inaccessible recovery can exclude operators on constrained devices and interrupt private work. Non-harm and excellence make actual task responsiveness and safe failure handling provider responsibilities, not only microbenchmark concerns.

IVSD-M007 preserves inherited budgets, first-use/outlier/failed records and distinct acknowledgement/completion/settlement intervals. Diagnose measured causes before optimizations; retain valid render state and operation authority across awaits. Offline updates cannot replace live work. Browser/native owners validate E13/E14 and R04/R05 on identified payloads/hosts; ordinary publication cannot qualify unrelated BuildCapture provenance.

Compact requires one global six-initial/ten-later owner across its sections,
not one allowance per section or a pagination/virtualization substitute.
Measure its whole page, scrolling/focus stability and eventual admission.
Guided keeps its own selected-section baseline; a passing result for one mode
cannot qualify the other. E19-E22 ground the r2 impact; runs remain unexecuted.

### IVSD-F008 - Quality claims need evidence without surveillance

The ambition to offer the best experience can encourage unqualified success claims or unnecessary analytics. Sidq and avoiding spying (Tajassus) require scoped evidence without collecting operators' answers. The provider controls study methods, telemetry defaults, published claims and follow-up.

IVSD-M008 retains zero product telemetry and uses synthetic task inputs with deliberately arranged studies. Report individual qualitative outcomes and missing evidence; do not infer population success rates from a small group. The steward arranges participant/AT/language access without implied messaging permission. E15 and R03 establish the evidence boundary; stakeholder and operational outcomes remain unreviewed.

## Recommendations

Retain all eight mitigations in the same plan. Reduce visual repetition without reducing required consent or truth. Reuse shared semantic authority and native adapters; the new layout does not require a new backend or global state framework.

Rejected alternatives: automatic secret consent, silent answer persistence, a duplicate state layer, performance-only acceptance and tiny-study population claims. For r2, pagination/fixed-height/virtualized Compact settings conflict with the user's chosen interaction; applying continuity/symbol presentation to Guided exceeds scope. Preserve both actual modes with one answer authority and explicit view-policy boundaries.

## Stakeholders

Novices need understandable choices; experienced operators need efficient search/editing and exact technical facts. Returning operators need preserved agency and recovery limits. Arabic and assistive-technology users need substantive access. Maintainers need bounded authoring guidance. Secret custodians and target administrators retain acceptance/security duties. ISLAMU stewards control release claims, resources and follow-up; external authors retain their expression and license rights.

## Common Overlooked Failures And Outcomes

Collapsing a group must not omit values. A stale correction must not edit another row. Canceling an operation must not resurrect output. A stripped profile must not look complete. A recovery recipient label must not imply verified recovery. A browser download must not claim protected save. Guided's legitimate section unmounting must not be removed to pass Compact continuity. Shared visual styles/policy must not change Guided or native behavior. A headless test, old screenshot or earlier report cannot qualify current scroll/accessibility/timing.
