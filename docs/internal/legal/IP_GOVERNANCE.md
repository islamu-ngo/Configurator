# Configurator IP and Dependency Governance

## Transfer authority

The Project Steward explicitly directed MIT transfer of ISLAMU-owned material
from Event into Configurator. The destination's existing MIT LICENSE remains
authoritative. Event's AGPL license and service covenant are not changed.
This transfer direction does not confer rights over third-party expression.

The foundation inventory records each selected tracked entry and its disposition.
Generic source knowledge is preserved and adapted; absent Event backend rules
are reference-only. Do not treat copied examples as permission to add backend,
database, host administration or authentication dependencies to the offline tool.

## External material

Use source-free functional specifications for externally informed implementation.
Keep source identities and access dates in provenance records, but do not ingest
incompatible source, expressive documentation, assets or source-derived designs.
Unclear rights block the affected transfer, not the rest of the work.

The 154 tracked entries under `.agents/skills/gitbook/resources/example-site`
are excluded: the Evolve Demo site-derived corpus has no verified transfer
license and includes tokenized GitBook assets. Project-native example guidance
replaces the corpus; do not reconstruct it from its URLs.

## Components and distribution

Third-party components retain their copyright, license conditions, disclaimers
and identity. Evaluate the actual resolved/published dependency graph, including
native assets and test-only packages, rather than inferring distribution rights
from central package declarations. Record exact versions, terms, provenance,
notices and approval before changing the closure.

The audited ISLAMU.Terminal.Gui downstream identity, fixed version, patch,
approval, assembly/closure ratchets, SBOM and notices remain intact. Its approved
archive is authoritative; NuGet pack's random OPC metadata prevents identical
ZIP rebuilds. Do not relax ratchets to accept a reconstructed archive.

git-cliff is an operator-supplied build-time renderer with a frozen toolchain
identity. Its binary is not installed by this transfer. A Configurator-owned
promotion authority must precede signed publication; source tooling does not
constitute trust activation.
