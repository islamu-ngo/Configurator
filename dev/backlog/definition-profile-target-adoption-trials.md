# Independent target adoption and stakeholder trials for definition v2

Last Updated: 2026-10-28 Europe/Brussels
Status: Required evidence follow-up before broader adoption/quality claims;
not performed or authorized by definition-standard qualification.
Owners: Configurator integration/client maintainers and each participating
target's maintainers; localization/accessibility reviewers and stakeholder
participants own their respective assessments.
Trigger: A named adopter or release claim needs target acceptance, usability,
translation, accessibility or Islamic responsibility evidence beyond synthetic
conformance and the exercised local clients.

## Outcome and evidence baseline

Run bounded, separately consented trials against the delivered v2 contract,
with explicit participants, target versions and success criteria. Core v2
admission/evaluation and selected recursive editing behavior are delivered.
This is not a place to move unfinished selected v2 behavior.

Qualification commit `55c951d` records Desktop 229 passed, all 19 real X11 guards
in both English and Arabic, and corrected Terminal package interaction.
That is local Linux qualification, not target-owned validator acceptance,
stakeholder usability, translation review, scholarly review, human assistive
technology or another host's protected-save proof. The
[v2 ADR](../../docs/internal/adr/ADR-definition-standard-v2.md) preserves the
architecture; [Operations](../../docs/internal/OPERATIONS.md) owns execution
evidence. Counts alone cannot close the gaps below.

## Boundaries and independent work

- Use synthetic or explicitly approved inputs and retain value-free results.
  Do not collect private operator answers, credentials, telemetry or screenshots
  containing restricted content as trial evidence.
- Preparation is not apply/admin authority. A target trial cannot deploy, write
  externally or broaden retrieval/publication authority without its own approval.
- [Event adoption](guided-configuration-delivery-event.md) remains Event-owned
  and Event-last, with independent target expected bytes and receipt/selector
  checks. Generic trials neither migrate Event nor close that program.
- [Native custody](cli-public-authoring-custody.md),
  [directory authority](core-directory-source-boundary.md),
  [other-host proof](configurator-native-host-evidence.md),
  [new formats](configuration-format-expansion.md) and
  [publication](publisher-definition-distribution.md) remain independently owned.
  New-host evidence must use that host; X11 results cannot substitute.
- Missing scholarly or stakeholder participation remains a documented gap.
  Engineering alignment with I-VSD is not a scholarly endorsement. Parent-owned
  I-VSD reconciliation is not replaced by this backlog.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each trial names its target/version, definition revision/digest, actual
   client/package, host, locale, participants/reviewer roles, approved input
   policy and bounded task scenarios before execution. Record unavailable
   participants or environments explicitly.
2. Target maintainers check newly prepared output with their own parser/validator
   and independently specified expected semantics/bytes, including rejection
   boundaries. Synthetic projection success and Configurator tests are distinct
   results, not target acceptance.
3. Actual adopter tasks exercise mixed fixed/typed maps, arrays, nullable and
   variant values, required/error recovery, omission/reset, deliberate suggestions,
   secret-deferred drafts and applicable help. Report defects against their
   existing owners instead of redefining required behavior as optional.
4. English/Arabic or other selected locale trials separately assess comprehension,
   fallback, RTL/technical text, keyboard/focus and relevant assistive technology.
   Equal output bytes prove output invariance, not translation or usability.
5. Stakeholder feedback and any qualified scholarly assessment retain method,
   scope, disagreements and evidence-limited disposition. No universal quality
   or religious approval is inferred from absent review.
6. A durable report separates executed results, failures and unverified claims,
   with concrete follow-up owners/triggers/acceptance. Only evidenced claims
   graduate to public/internal guidance; unavailable work remains open rather
   than being closed by a build, test total or this document.
